# Safety disclaimers

## General

OCTA FIT is an educational graduation project. It does not replace professional medical, physiotherapy, or coaching advice.

## AI Coach

Fitness-oriented assistant. Does not prescribe medications or diagnose disease. Users with medical conditions should consult qualified professionals.

## AI Physiotherapist

Provides **educational preliminary guidance** with cautious language, uncertainty wording, and urgent-care / red-flag redirection where applicable. It does **not**:

- Give definitive diagnoses
- Recommend medication dosing
- Replace licensed physiotherapy or emergency care

## Motion Analyzer

Provides **pose-estimation-based educational movement analysis** for supported demonstration exercises. It does **not** provide medical-grade movement diagnosis. Unsupported exercises and invalid videos should fail safely.

## Data

Do not upload real patient medical records. Prefer synthetic demo videos for Motion Analyzer.
